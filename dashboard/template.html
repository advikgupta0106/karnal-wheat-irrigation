Design concept of a farmer-facing app. It replays archived weather for Karnal up to __ASOF__. It is not a live system. Tomorrow's crop water use is estimated with the persistence rule (tomorrow = today), not our ML model. This is a prototype, not expert farm advice.

Karnal Wheat Irrigation Advisor: design concept

Data-driven. Efficient. Future-ready.

Type into My Field (area, soil, variety, sowing date, last irrigation) or Weather (today's ET0, expected rain) and every screen updates. Tomorrow's crop water loss is assumed equal to today's.

Sources: Open-Meteo (ERA5) and NASA POWER daily data to __ASOF__. ET0 is calculated (FAO-56 Penman-Monteith), not measured in a field. Soil sets the trigger deficit (sandy loam 40, loam 60, clay loam 80 mm, the notebook's scenario range). Variety is a label only until stage dates per variety are available.
