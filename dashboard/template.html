Karnal Wheat Irrigation Advisor: design concept

Data-driven. Efficient. Future-ready.

Type into My Field (area, trigger scenario, sowing date, last irrigation) or Weather (today's ET0, expected rain) and every screen updates. Tomorrow's ET0 comes from our 11-feature linear regression model.

Sources: Open-Meteo (ERA5) and NASA POWER daily data to __ASOF__. ET0 is calculated (FAO-56 Penman-Monteith), not measured in a field. The trigger deficit is a scenario (40, 60 or 80 mm, the notebook's range), not a soil measurement: we have no soil texture or rooting-depth data for Karnal.
