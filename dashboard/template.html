Karnal Wheat Irrigation Predictor

Data-driven. Efficient. Future-ready.

Use the tabs at the bottom of the phone, or tap a card, to move between screens. Enter your field on My Field and today's weather and expected rain on Weather. Tomorrow's crop water loss is predicted by the notebook's AI model (linear regression on today's weather).

Sources: Open-Meteo (ERA5) and NASA POWER daily data to . ET0 is calculated (FAO-56 Penman-Monteith), not measured in a field. Tomorrow's ET0 comes from the notebook's linear regression (11 features), refitted by build.py, which checks the test error matches the notebook. Soil sets the trigger deficit (sandy loam 40, loam 60, clay loam 80 mm, the notebook's scenario range). Variety is a label only until stage dates per variety are available.
